# Lily Palette

* `#1A1310` Ink. Where the leaves hide.
* `#382C26` Dark Roast. The deep background.
* `#5E4D42` Cocoa Dust. The soft middle of the sky.
* `#8F7B6A` Faded Tea. Stems and shadows.
* `#C9BAA6` Old Paper. The edge of a petal.
* `#EFE7DA` Lily Cream. The brightest bloom.
